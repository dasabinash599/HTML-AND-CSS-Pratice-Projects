About Our College

Nalanda Institute Of Technology is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing high-quality education and training in various fields of technology. Our mission is to empower students with the knowledge and skills they need to succeed in their careers.

Our Courses

B.Tech in Computer Science

Our B.Tech program in Computer Science provides students with a strong foundation in computer science principles and practices.

B.Tech in Electronics

Our B.Tech program in Electronics focuses on the study of electronic devices, circuits, and systems.

B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering

Our B.Tech program in Mechanical Engineering covers the design, analysis, and manufacturing of mechanical systems.

B.Tech in Civil Engineering

Our B.Tech program in Civil Engineering focuses on the design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure projects.

B.Tech in Electrical Engineering

Our B.Tech program in Electrical Engineering covers the study of electrical systems, power generation, and distribution.

B.Tech in Information Technology

Our B.Tech program in Information Technology focuses on the study of computer systems, software development, and networking.
